ABOUT THIS FEEDBACK BUILD

A visual model of two different kinds of textual complexity

Internal Consistency uses the existing 120-item research catalog. Arc height approximates elapsed modeled working days; during playback a detail can remain an incomplete thread until its matching passage is reached.

Hebrew Poetics uses Donald W. Parry’s Poetic Parallelisms in the Book of Mormon index. The archive contains 1,618 indexed occurrences across 23 exact form labels, grouped here into five visual families for legibility.

Parry’s category is broader than formal poetry: parallel and repetitive forms occur in narrative, sermons, prophecy, letters, prayers, legal texts, and other genres. The 60-day dictation timeline is a workload model, not a reconstructed historical calendar.
