How to read this layer
The definitions and passage references come from Donald W. Parry’s index of poetic forms. “Compact” versus “extended/complex” is a visualization teaching heuristic based primarily on indexed passage span; it is not a complexity ranking supplied by Parry.
Chiasmus weighting
Chiasmus is intentionally weighted far more heavily than the other forms. The score grows superlinearly with absolute span, then gets an additional chapter-coverage bonus. This makes large structures such as Alma 36 visually dominant.
